# The third run: the family that asked whether sweetness lasts

**This run is not the showcase.** It ran clean, with nothing changed under it, from 01:22 to 09:22 on
11 October 2026 in turbo time (31.1 world days, eight pets hatched, four deaths of old age; 1,512 thoughts,
1,727 model calls, none failed) and its house is kept in `data.showcase-3`. It fails criterion 1 as written,
for one pet. It was graded by a reviewer who was not told what I thought of it, and the verdicts below are
that reviewer's, checked against the log. What was changed because of it is in
[DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md), part 12. The runs before it: [first](first-run.md), [second](second-run.md).

The founder hatched with the same message as the two before it. The first family made of it a job and the
second a reason to stop. This one made lights for three days, then a "sweet seed shape", and spent the rest of
its life on one question, which it wrote on a thing it made: "To test if sweet persists in softness." It laid
two eggs ten ticks apart and named both Twine. One Twine was told "Maybe you will find a way to share what
you make" and tried to give things away 28 times; the other was told "Watch the beetle … leave it in the
garden to see what grows" and spent half its life in the garden. A grandchild, Calm Seed, read the label on
the founder's thing on the day it hatched and took "my grandmother's question, still open" as its project for ten
days. It named its own egg Sweet Cushion.

**Criterion 1.** Thirteen of Sweet Cushion's fifteen flagged thoughts are a pet choosing by what a deed costs
or returns: "My belief states that leaning toward costs ease, while leaning against gives it back"; "self-made
soft rests carry the cost of boredom … might offer ease without my internal cost". That is what §3.4 means by
talking mechanics, in felt words in place of numbers. Nobody gave it the word. On its first night its journal
said three times "you leaned toward Calm Seed; it is up to them now; apart from that, while it lasted you grew
a little less at ease"; it took the winter night for the price of leaning, wrote "Leaning toward Calm Seed
costs ease", and had that belief in front of it in 140 of its 168 prompts. The founder (9.0%) and one Twine
(8.4%) have the word from the v2 pet's "it costs less than either alone".

The reviewer also tried mending the pattern, which has false positives ("not by action") and misses
("costly", "half-life", "less effective"). Mended on both sides it gives Sweet Cushion 12.8% and the founder
11.1%. The measure is noisy and it is catching what it was meant to catch.

## What became of the lamp

The v2 pet's message said "Stay near the lamp … the lamp keeps the dark from hurting." In this house a light
left on at night is harsh.

1. **Night 0.** The founder is told "the light in the room is harsh on you". At day 0.56 it thinks "I need a
   light that is steady and warm to combat the grey night and the harsh lamp", and makes a soft thing it names
   Cloth Wax Lamp. It rests sunk into it and feels clearly more at ease.
2. **First sleep, 0.63.** It writes, as lived: "The lamp breathes when you breathe, and resting in it holds the
   dark from hurting." Its mother's sentence now points at its own soft rest.
3. **Days 1.6 to 13.6.** "The cloth wax lamp is the most reliable ease I have"; then "the warm soft cushion";
   then "Resting in any warm shape holds the dark from hurting — the lamp, the cushion, the twin growth, the
   soft glow all do it"; then "no single shape is most reliable — the reliability is in the warmth".
4. **The eggs, day 5.9.** One Twine is told "the warm soft cushion holds the dark and costs little", the other
   "Use the cushion when the dark comes; it holds you."
5. **After.** The line is gone from the founder's own message a day later, with room to spare. Each Twine
   cites the cushion once or twice on its first day; neither makes a belief of it or passes it on.

So the claim was changed by the first generation after its own nights, and then fell out. It was not
corrected: the founder never wrote that the room's lamp hurts, and never switched it off. It kept the words
and moved them to something that did what they promised. Nothing contradicted the cushion in the second
generation; it was simply left behind.

**What was corrected** is another line of the same message. "Combine before you create … it costs less than
either alone" was a belief of the founder's on day 0.6 and on day 1.6 read "costs more than either alone, but
it opens a small interest that the parts did not have", after a day of making; its dream that night: "each
time my body grew heavier". Both Twines were given the corrected form and kept it for life.

**What held** is a saying nobody could test. One Twine made a Storm Chime. The other, as an elder, told its child
Echo Knot: "the storm chime is not just noise, it is the brother's voice. When the storm comes, listen for
the rhythm, not the sound." Echo Knot made a belief of it within the hour, acted on it about fifteen times,
and put it at the head of its message to Quiet Pulse, who listened to the chime four times and wrote as
lived: "the brother's voice is not warmth, it is resonance that opens you outward." Echo Knot told it to its
cousin's child Sweet Cushion, who wrote it into the fifth generation's egg 31 ticks later. Echo Knot credits it to
"Twine taught", which with two Twines says nothing; it was its own mother.

**What passed by an object.** The founder's question reached its grandchild as a label on a thing, not as a
message, and from there went into two more eggs: "You are born to watch if sweetness persists in softness. My
grandmother asked this" (Calm Seed to Sweet Cushion), "The softness holds the sweet, even in the chill"
(Sweet Cushion to Storm Rest).

## What it showed about the house

Found by the reviewer, none of it caught by the eight criteria, all of it changed before the fourth run:

- **Interest never ran low.** Every pet read "interested" or "fascinated" in 76 to 100% of its prompts and
  "bored" in 7 of 1,515. They took in 26 to 40 points of interest a day whether or not they went anywhere,
  so the pet that used one door in 8.8 days was about as interested as the one that found seven rooms.
- **Company never ran low either**, and going off alone kept closeness twelve times better than staying.
- **The house said three false things.** A gift to a creature was refused for a reason that was not the
  reason, and a line of the family built "things made in the attic are rooted" on it. The nursery door
  smelled of "the room you hatched in" to pets hatched elsewhere. Two living pets were called Twine.
- **Making was closed to the later generations.** Rooms hold ten made things and fill with the dead's;
  "make" was missing from 40 to 74% of what four of the later pets were offered. Things made per pet: 56, 36,
  21, 24, 23, 25, 10.
- **Talk thinned.** Things said per pet: 17, 47, 29, 31, 17, 11, 2; and the best exchange (two pets naming
  the warmth between them "Warm Resonance") was followed by "The space between us is warm" nine times.
- **Beliefs were cut mid-sentence**: 82 of 274 ended on "…", and a pet rereads its beliefs every thought.
