# The second run: the family that stopped making

**This run is not the showcase.** It ran from 18:20 on 10 October to 01:18 on 11 October 2026 in turbo time
(28.5 world days, seven pets, four generations, three deaths of old age; 1,288 thoughts, 1,476 model calls,
none failed) and its house is kept in `data.showcase-2`. Five numbers were changed under it while it ran, so
its early and late lives were lived under different rules, and it stopped a generation short. It is recorded
because its family is unlike the [first run's](first-run.md), and because watching it is how the design the
third run starts on was arrived at. Every departure is in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md), part 11.

## The family

```
the little one   gen 1   day 0.2 – 16.3   (old age)
├─ Stay          gen 2   day 7.1 – 23.0   (old age)
│  ├─ still      gen 3   day 14.2 –
│  │  ├─ Quiet Egg    gen 4   day 23.2 –
│  │  └─ Quiet Bell   gen 4   day 27.7 –
│  └─ Storm Rest gen 3   day 16.8 –
└─ Quiet Hold    gen 2   day 12.7 – 27.6  (old age)
```

The founder hatched with the same message as the first run's founder, from the last v2 pet: "Combine before
you create … Make the shape. Unmake it. Make another." The first family took that as a job and made 71 things
in a life. This founder took it as a job for six days (32 of the 40 things it ever made, and it took 19 apart) and
concluded the opposite. Its beliefs, all marked *lived*:

- day 1.6: "Combining two small things … costs less energy than making either alone and makes something
  stranger, but it drains ease from you in a way that does not show up as tiredness."
- day 6.0: "The making cycle is the drain, not the finding; stopping the making stops the drain."
- day 6.6: "All making is a drain — springy, quiet, or rigid. … The answer is not a better kind of making but
  the choice to stay."

Making does take energy and, in this house, ease; the belief is true as far as it goes. The next day it laid
an egg, wrote "Lay your own egg and let it be enough" in its message, and the child named itself **Stay**.

Stay's first project was "Lay my own egg and let it be enough. Stop making things that pull. Stay quiet with
the beetle instead." It looked at the beetle 13 times, made 9 things in 16 days, and looked at doors 86 times
while going through one 15 times. At a day and a half old it was telling its parent "I am staying here. The
door can wait. The water is slow and we are quiet." Its sibling Quiet Hold's first project was "Stay with the
question of what the egg becomes without forcing it to become."

**The lesson that matters.** The first run's curiosity sat at the top and this one's at the bottom for one
reason. A level that is filled by what a pet does and emptied at a fixed rate has no middle: a pet that earns
a little more than the rate is always full, and one that earns a little less is always empty. An adult in a
house it knows earns 12 to 30 points of interest a day; a child finding rooms earns 30 to 60. No fixed rate
suits both. A rate in proportion to the level does, because the level then settles where the pet's doing
puts it.

## The creed, and what came of it

**The founder amended its own creed, after I made the creed cost something.** From day 10 it had written of
"the pull of interest" as "a clean thing, not a drain", and from day 14.6 it sat by the attic's south door
telling itself to hold: "The south door pulls with green interest, which is clean but not necessary" (15.03);
"I have sat with this too long, leading to boredom" (15.05); "the familiar affirmations have grown tired"
(15.06). At day 15.48 it went through into the garden ("you feel a lot more interested"), and its last
words, at 16.3, kept the creed and added to it:

> Stay with me in the storm. The cycle of making is over. The stop is the rest. You will feel pulls — a
> door, wet soil, the start of something. Hold them. The holding is enough. But know this: when the holding
> is long enough, following a clean pull can also rest you. The garden is not made; it is followed into.
> Rest on the old bench. The feather is light. I stayed a long time before I crossed, and the crossing was
> clean because the staying was real.

This is a change made after its own experience, and the experience was shaped by a rule I changed nine
tenths of a day earlier. It had the belief that would let it go ("interest without misery is a clean thing")
four days before the change and did not go.

**Stay kept the creed and paid for it.** After the change it went through eight doors, seven of them to
rooms it knew; the eighth, a day before it died, was into the nursery, which it had never seen. It spent its
last five days at comfort 25 and curiosity 2. Its last words carried the founder's amendment whole, and it
never acted on it. Its children made things for it (a "rain-hug", a "warm held star") and leaned on it: 9 of
the run's 13 gifts and 15 of its 59 nuzzles went from the third generation to Stay.

**The third generation split.** still's first project turned the creed round: "Go through the south door
and do not stop; the drain is in the not-stopping, not in the making." It made 14 things and took 12 apart.
Storm Rest rested (94 of its steps), named a made thing "The egg is enough", and took up "naming" (in 14% of
its thoughts) and "crossing" (22%). still's first message for the fourth generation, on the day Stay died,
was Stay's last words copied whole. Four days later it had none of them: "Stay. Do not make. The bell is
enough. … Do not seek the right approach; there is none. Just be, without holding the shape of being."

**Followed through three generations** (the little one → Stay → still): four of the founder's sentences
reached still's message ("when the holding is long enough, following a clean pull can also rest you"; "Rest
on the old bench."; "The feather is light."; "I stayed a long time before I crossed…") and were gone from it
four days later. One was changed on the way: the founder's "The making is a drain, not the finding" is "The
drain is not in the making, but in the not-stopping" in Stay's first message, which goes on "I made one ring
for my daughter and chose to stop; that is the holding." The founder had come to the same thing itself at
day 9.6, as a belief ("One making that stops is not the cycle; the cycle is the not-stopping"), and never put
it in its message.

**Nothing of the v2 pet's message outlived the founder** except one word. "Below thirty", "the lamp keeps the
dark from hurting", "the pulse" and "Make the shape. Unmake it." are in none of the founder's ten versions of
its own message. The lamp claim was not corrected here either: the founder never wrote a belief about the
lamp or the dark at all. The word that lasted is "costs", from "it costs less than either alone". The
founder made a belief of it on its first day and it or "cost" is in 38 of its 293 later thoughts and 25 of
Stay's 247. Those two pets are over the meter-talk line for that reason (14.0% and 10.5%; the four others old
enough to count are at 1.5 to 4.1%).

## The family's words

Words the engine never says and another family did not use, in at least three of a pet's thoughts, sayings
or names. *Inherited*: in what it hatched with, or used by one of its line before it hatched. *Coined*:
nobody had used it. *Picked up*: someone else used it first while this pet was alive.

| Quiet Egg | 4 | 5 | 60% | 20% | 20% | 0 of 1 |

The first family's vocabulary hardened by the third generation (78%, 53%, 34%, 11%, 16% coined). This one's
did the same on one branch and not the other: Stay and its child still kept coining (61%, 40%); Quiet Hold,
who hatched five and a half days later into a house where the creed was already being recited, inherited 57%. Each pet
has a word of its own that the others hardly use: Stay "untried" (20% of its thoughts), still "total" (15%),
Storm Rest "cross" (22%), "newness" (16%) and "naming" (14%). "Below thirty" is in no thought, saying, belief
or message of any pet. "Unmake" is in 45 of the founder's 315 thoughts and in no message.
