# Pet — v3.5.1 spec: the hub

Everything decided on the evening of 13 October about the machine room, in one place. It follows the machine
room options Code made (A, B, C and the mix D). SPEC-v3 to v3.5 still hold. Departures go in DECISIONS.md.

**How to work:** test on the live house with a backup of `data/` first; short checks; near-misses don't
block; two helpers at most; don't change the spec mid-build, ask instead. Commits as Intranet-Explorer, a
Claude co-author trailer is fine. README.md is the front page: leave it.

## 1. The machine room

**Why:** the vending machine is the biggest thing in the pets' lives and should look it, and the useful things
in the house should be in one place the pets come and go from.

- A tenth room, "the machine room", off the storeroom, found like any other room (a moment when it is).
- **The machine is option D, "The Works"**: A's arcade cabinet, B's hopper and glass tubes, C's belt. Read left
  to right: hopper, cabinet with six lit windows showing what's for sale, the belt out of its side, tubes up into
  the walls. The hopper gulps, light runs through the tubes, the chosen window lights, the belt throws the item
  to the pet on the landing pad.
  - **Taller:** nearly to the top of the wall.
  - **The sign:** the marquee's "AiVAOS" text means nothing. A plain glowing sign: a simple symbol or the
    room's name.
- The vending machine and its stock move here from the storeroom. "Stock the machine" keeps working.
- Pets carry trade-ins here and materials back; what they carry shows in their paws.

## 2. The ticker

- A dot-matrix LED sign on the wall, drawn in code, like old scrolling ad signs.
- It shows **the time, the forecast and house headlines** (inventions, discoveries, births, deaths, things
  built, a statue finished). **No outside news.**
- Between messages, small pixel animations in the style of a pinball machine's dot display: sun, cloud, rain,
  snow, fire for a cold night, moon at night, a star burst for a discovery.
- Pets can read it: the forecast reaches them from here, and so do the headlines.

## 3. The computers

- Two or three old computer terminals that search the offline Simple English Wikipedia, so several pets can
  look things up at once (the book allows one read per pet per hour).
- When a pet uses one, the screen shows what it typed and the text scrolling, green or amber. Tyler can read
  what they're reading.
- **The heavy book stays in the attic as a relic.** Lean learned to make the house's first trowel from it.
  Pets can still read it there.

## 4. The founder's statue

- Room in the centre of the hub for a statue.
- When the hub is first found, the eldest pet gets a want to honour the first one there. The family builds it
  as a lasting project (v3.5 §4) from stone, over a world day or two, so Tyler can watch it go up.
- The statue is the founder's own body drawn in stone (from its genome, through the parts library). Its plaque
  carries a name the family chooses (the founder was never named; it's "the little one" in the records) and the
  founder's last words. Clicking it opens the founder's page once the UI redesign exists.
- Each family may honour its own founder: the second family came from the foundling egg.
- Later: more statues for whoever a family decides mattered (the first inventor, the first tool-maker), so the
  hub becomes a hall of ancestors. Not now.

## 5. The radio becomes a real station

- The radio moves to the hub.
- When it's on, the page plays a real internet radio station for Tyler. The engine reads the station's
  now-playing information, so pets perceive the song and its mood ("the radio is playing something slow and
  dreamy: '…'") and react as they do to the visitor's music: dancing, humming, settling.
- "Their radio" in the header switches stations. Pick a few, mellow to lively, that allow their stream to be
  played on a personal page (SomaFM is a candidate). Check each station's terms first.
- Drop the news, spoken-forecast and "On this day" sources. The forecast is on the ticker now.
